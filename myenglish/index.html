Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

Developer Contact: arakelyan.sep@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how MyEnglish ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app. MyEnglish teaches English to Armenian-speaking learners aged 12 and over. We collect only what the app needs to work.

Information we collect and how we use it

Sign-in. You sign in with your Google account through Google Firebase Authentication. Firebase Authentication receives your Google account identifier and email address so it can create and secure your account. We do not store your real name, email address, or photo in the app's learning profile.

Learning profile. To save your progress across devices, we store in Google Firebase Realtime Database: the nickname you choose, your buddy character and its accessories, your lesson and word progress, XP, streak, and the date you were last active.

Age and parental consent. Before leagues are enabled, the app asks for your birth year. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country, the app asks for a parent's or guardian's email address so that the parent can confirm consent. The parent's email is used only for this confirmation.

Leagues. If you take part in weekly leagues, your nickname, buddy, and weekly XP are visible to other learners in the same league group. No real names, photos, chat, or free text are shown to other users.

On your device. Settings such as theme and daily reminder time are stored only on your device. Daily reminders are local notifications created by the app on your device. English pronunciation may use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.

No ads, no tracking. MyEnglish does not show advertising, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not include analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.

Support. When you contact us, we use your email address and the information in your message only to respond to your request.

Who receives your information

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) stores your account and learning profile. Our database is hosted in the European Union (europe-west1). See the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In and Google Play Services are used to sign you in and to distribute and update the app. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Email provider (Gmail) processes support messages you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. Our service providers may process information on servers outside your country. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses used by Google.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database access rules allow each signed-in user to read and change only their own profile and progress; only league entries (nickname, buddy, weekly XP) are readable by other signed-in learners.

How long we keep information

  • Account and learning profile are kept while your account exists and are deleted when you delete your account.
  • League entries belong to a weekly league and are removed when you leave a league or delete your account.
  • On-device settings stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Profile and choose delete account. This removes your learning profile, progress, and sign-in account. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing arakelyan.sep@gmail.com from any address; tell us your nickname so we can find your account.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict certain uses, withdraw consent, or complain to your local privacy regulator. To exercise a right, contact us at arakelyan.sep@gmail.com.

Where required by law, we process your profile and progress to provide the service you asked for, and we rely on parental consent where the law requires it for younger users.

Children’s privacy

MyEnglish is designed for learners aged 12 and over. It collects no real names or photos and has no chat. Where the law requires parental consent for a user's age, leagues stay disabled until a parent or guardian confirms. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 12. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information without your consent, contact us at arakelyan.sep@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When we update this policy, we will post the revised version on this page and change the effective date.
