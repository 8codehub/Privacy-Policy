Delete Your Account

Developer: Hovsep Arakelyan

Delete your profile from the app settings:

  1. Open the MyEnglish app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (Պրոֆիլ), where the app settings are.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account (Ջնջել հաշիվը).
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete (Ջնջել).

Your account and all its data are deleted right away.

Delete only your data and keep your account:

  1. Open the MyEnglish app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (Պրոֆիլ), where the app settings are.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete data (Ջնջել տվյալները).
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete (Ջնջել).

Your nickname, buddy, progress, XP, streak, birth year, parent email and league entries are deleted right away. Your Google sign-in account stays, and you start the course again from the beginning.

If the app asks you to sign in again for security, sign out, sign back in with the same Google account, and repeat the steps.

No access to the app?

You can ask for either option (full account deletion or data only) by email. Email arakelyan.sep@gmail.com with the subject "Delete MyEnglish account" and include your nickname or the Google account email you signed in with. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Your learning profile: nickname, buddy and accessories, birth year, parent email
  • Your progress: lessons, words, XP, streak
  • Your league entries

What is kept

Nothing is kept on our servers after deletion. Settings stored only on your device (theme, reminder time) are removed when you uninstall the app. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.
