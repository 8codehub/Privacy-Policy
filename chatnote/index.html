NoteIt · Simple Notes

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information NoteIt | Simple Notes (the "App", formerly ChatNote) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. In short: your notes stay on your device, there is no account to create, the App shows no ads, and we never sell your data.

3. What Stays on Your Device

Everything you create in the App is stored only on your device: notes, folders, images, videos, voice recordings, drawings, reminders and settings. We do not upload it to our servers, and we cannot see it.

4. Information We Collect

To keep the App working well, we use Google Firebase to collect a limited amount of technical information:

  • Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics): which features are used, for example that a note was added, search was opened, or a read-aloud language was downloaded, together with internal values such as a folder's ID, a count of notes, or the language and voice chosen for read aloud. This never includes the content of your notes, folder names, attachments or recordings. Firebase also records general device and app information and assigns an identifier to the installation; it does not identify you personally.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): if the App crashes, technical details such as the device model, Android version, App version and where the error happened, so we can fix it.
  • Notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging): an identifier that lets us send news and update notices to the App.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or the content of anything you write or record.

5. How We Use Information

  • To keep the App running and fix errors.
  • To understand which features are useful and improve them.
  • To send notifications you have allowed.

6. Internet Connections the App Makes

Besides Firebase, the App connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • Link previews: when a note contains a link, the App loads that web page to show its title and image. The website receives your device's IP address, as with any page you open. You can turn this off in Settings › Link previews.
  • Read aloud: speech is generated on your device and the text of your notes is never sent anywhere. When you choose a language for the first time, its voice files are downloaded from GitHub, which receives your device's IP address and standard request information (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

7. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. The only service that receives data from the App is Google Firebase, as described above, under the Google Privacy Policy. Your notes leave the App only when you choose to share or export them, for example as a PDF.

8. Backup and Security

  • Device backup: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include the App's data in your Google account backup. This is handled by Google, and you can turn it off in your device settings.
  • App lock: you can protect the App with a PIN or fingerprint. Your fingerprint is checked by Android; the App never receives or stores it.
  • Notes are stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read. They are not additionally encrypted by the App.

9. Permissions

The App asks only for what its features need, and you can refuse or revoke any of them in your device settings:

  • Camera: to take photos and videos for your notes.
  • Microphone: to record voice notes.
  • Notifications: for reminders, news and update notices, and to show what is playing while a note is read aloud.
  • Internet: for the connections described in sections 4 and 6.
  • Media playback in the background: only while a note is being read aloud, so reading continues with the screen locked. It stops when reading ends.
  • Fingerprint: only if you turn on app lock.

10. Deleting Your Data

  • Delete individual notes or folders in the App.
  • Delete everything at once in Settings › Clear app data.
  • Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device.

Usage statistics and crash reports are kept by Firebase for a limited time under Google's retention policies. To ask us to delete them, email help.chatnote@gmail.com.

11. Advertising

The App does not show ads.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
