Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how read.ing ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the app.

How we use your information

Reading data. Your books, documents, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, and preferences are stored locally on your device so the app can open your content, remember where you left off, and keep your settings. We do not upload this data to our servers, and we do not read or analyze your content.

Accounts and sync. read.ing does not require an account or sign-in. We do not collect names, usernames, or passwords, and we do not sync your reading data to our own servers. If you use your device's system backup (for example, Android or Google backup), that backup is handled by your device provider under its own privacy policy.

App performance, analytics, and advertising. To keep the app stable and improve it, we use Google Firebase to collect diagnostic and usage information, such as crash logs, device model, operating system version, app version, app instance identifiers, and general app activity (for example, which screens are used). This information is not used to identify you personally. The app does not show advertising and does not use your information for targeted advertising.

Support. When you contact us, we use your email address and the information you include in your message to respond and resolve your request.

Who receives your information

We share information only with service providers that help us run the app:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) receives the diagnostic and usage information described above to provide crash reporting and usage statistics. See the Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services is used to distribute and update the app. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Email provider (Gmail) processes support messages you send us.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

Our service providers may process information on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses used by Google.

How long we keep information

  • Reading data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics data is retained by Firebase for up to 14 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and handle any follow-up, then deleted.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can remove reading data at any time by deleting books, bookmarks, or notes inside the app, or by going to Settings > Apps > read.ing > Storage > Clear data on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. You can limit advertising identifiers on Android under Settings > Google > Ads. Because the app has no accounts, there is no account to delete.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict certain uses, withdraw consent, or complain to your local privacy regulator. To exercise a right, contact us at arakelyan.sep@gmail.com.

Where required by law, we rely on our legitimate interests to process diagnostic and usage data to keep the app secure and working, and on your request to process support messages. Providing personal information is not required to use the app's reading features.

Children’s privacy

read.ing is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us at arakelyan.sep@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

When we update this policy, we will post the revised version and change the effective date.
